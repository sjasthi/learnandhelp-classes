## About This Repository

This repository contains the **syllabi and weekly planners** for courses
offered by **Learn and Help**.

The purpose of this repository is to provide students, parents,
prospective students, and the community with an overview of our courses,
including learning objectives, topics covered, prerequisites, and
week-by-week learning plans.

## Course Materials

This repository contains publicly available course information such as:

-   Course syllabi
-   Weekly planners
-   Course schedules
-   Learning objectives
-   Prerequisites and recommended preparation

### Registered Student Resources

Detailed curriculum materials---including **PowerPoint presentations,
quizzes, assignments, exercises, sample programs, projects, and other
learning resources**---are available through **Google Classroom** or
private GitHub repositories.

> 🔒 **Access to these detailed curriculum resources is limited to
> registered Learn and Help students.**

## Our Courses

Learn and Help offers courses in areas such as:

-   Python Programming
-   Data Science
-   Machine Learning
-   Artificial Intelligence & LLMs
-   AP Computer Science
-   AI-Assisted Web Application Development
-   Computational Thinking
-   Technology for Civic and Social Impact

Course offerings may vary by academic year.

## About Learn and Help

**Learn and Help** is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to
teaching programming, artificial intelligence, and computational
thinking while inspiring students to use their skills to make a positive
impact.

Our educational programs combine **learning with giving**. Proceeds from
our programs support educational initiatives for underserved students
and communities.

### Our Philosophy

**Learn. Create. Give Back.**

We want students not only to learn technology, but also to use what they
learn to **create solutions and make an impact**.
